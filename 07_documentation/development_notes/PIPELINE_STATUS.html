(A) DEFECT, inference-changing: the shore I/E likelihood was on the wrong unit. The shore I/E stream asserts IE_obs ~ lognormal(log(lambda_E * L), sigma_IE). That was correct while shore ran on crabber-hours: lambda_E is crabbers present and L was L_effective in HOURS. The v7.7 move to gear-deployments replaced L with tau_shore (a dimensionless turnover, ~1.7) but left the OBSERVATION as ie_crabber_hours, so from v7.7 until now the model has been comparing crabber-HOURS against a predicted lambda_E * tau = crabber-TRIPS. On a typical WDF20 survey day that is ~331 observed against ~80 predicted, a ~4x scale mismatch the gear-count stream then has to absorb. This is very likely the mechanism behind the standing, unexplained shore all-gear sigma_IE ~ 1.07 (GR-9), which was previously described only as “the two streams disagree by ~e^1 at the tails”. The boat stream was already correct (F2 put it on trips x turnover). Fixed by making the observation follow the effort unit through bss_effort_spec()$ie_obs_col (deployments -> ie_trips, the crabber arrival count already computed and previously unused; time units -> ie_crabber_hours), in both preps, so the pair cannot drift again. ie_shore_obs_unit = "crabber_hours" reproduces the old behaviour. This MOVES the shore number and is the single change in this batch that should be isolated in its own run, against the 2026-08-04 production baseline. Two consequences to watch: the shore I/E stream becomes genuinely informative about tau_shore (as the boat’s is about tau_boat) instead of fighting the gear counts, and sigma_IE should fall sharply if the diagnosis is right. If it does not, the GR-9 tension is something else and the item stays open.

(8) OSP crab-only counts as a HARD LOWER BOUND on f. OSP does not record combo trips (a boat that crabs AND fishes another fishery is labelled by the other fishery), so its crab-only share bounds f from below and is not an estimate of it. Treating it as an estimate would bias the boat harvest DOWN by exactly the combo rate, on precisely the halibut and tuna days where that rate is highest. The bound is therefore structural:

f_lower[k]       ~ Beta(1,1)
osp_crab_only[i] ~ BetaBinomial(osp_total[i], f_lower[k(i)] * kappa, (1 - f_lower[k(i)]) * kappa)
f[k]             = f_lower[k] + (1 - f_lower[k]) * theta[k]

The likelihood is per OSP DAY and beta-binomial, not a binomial on the stratum sum. A binomial on the sum would treat every boat-day as an independent trial (~150 days x ~50 boats -> n ~ 7,500, f_lower SD ~ 0.005), which ~150 correlated daily observations do not support; kappa is estimated from the spread of the daily shares instead. Because the bound is HARD and f multiplies the boat linearly, an over-precise f_lower would push f up with unearned confidence - the same false-precision failure the weather module was rejected for.

theta[k] is the share of the NOT-crab-labelled boats that were also crabbing. Identifiability, stated plainly: OSP alone identifies f_lower, never theta - the counts cannot separate “few crab boats” from “many combo trips”. theta rests on crab_fraction_combo_share (default 0.15, a PLACEHOLDER with exactly the standing f = 0.3 had before the pilot) until the WPT/WBL egress classification covers the same stratum, at which point the egress Binomial n_crab ~ Binomial(n_total, f) pins f and hence theta. That is the operational reason to schedule egress classification days on days OSP is in port rather than treating the two as independent efforts. Degradation is EXACT, not approximate: with no OSP classification in a stratum, f_lower is pinned to 0, f = theta, and R hands Stan the ordinary f prior for theta instead of the combo prior, so the posterior is bit-identical to the pre-batch model. Both new terms sit on OBSERVED boat counts, never on the latent effort, so f stays in generated quantities only and the validated Phase 2/3 properties survive: the boat is exactly linear in f, model CPUE is invariant. Ships use_osp_crab_lower = FALSE, for the same reason everything else behaviour-changing here ships off: a TRUE default would move the boat harvest on the same run that first read the new column, and at crab_fraction_strata = "none" much of that move would come from the combo-share PLACEHOLDER rather than from OSP’s data. It is additionally inert until the crab-only column exists in WBL_boat_counts.xlsx (osp_crab_only_col, default WestportCrabOnlyEffort), and a stratum below crab_fraction_osp_min_obs does not bind. The reader gains f_lower_out and osp_f_kappa_out alongside f_crab_out, so the bound, its day-to-day spread, and the level are reported separately.

The model change, improvement 2.1: a SHARED turnover. Both .stan models gain shared_tau, shared_tau_prior_mu, shared_tau_prior_sigma and shared_tau_sigma in the data block, and a vector<lower=0>[shared_tau] tau_bar parameter. With shared_tau = 1, L[d] = tau_bar * exp(shared_tau_sigma * L_raw[d]): one estimated level that every observed day informs, with a fixed day-to-day spread around it, replacing the D independent per-day anchors that made the 148-day OSP stream unable to move the boat turnover off its 1.2 prior. shared_tau_sigma is DATA rather than a parameter on purpose: the question is where the shared LEVEL goes, and estimating the spread at the same time lets the two trade off against each other on a series where most days are unobserved.

It ships FALSE, and its OFF path is verified bit-identical. tau_bar is zero-size when the feature is off, the same pattern that kept the gear track bit-identical through the 2026-08-25 batch. 5. Cross-cutting principles and lessons

  • Time-denominated effort is invalid for pot and trap gear. Catch is sub-linear in soak time (saturation beta ~0.13-0.27), so crabber-hours and gear-hours both fail the linearity test; gear-deployments is the harvest-unbiased unit. The pipeline now measures this every run (cpue_linearity_*.csv, cpue_saturation_*.csv), so any new gear type or population must be run through those before its totals are trusted.
  • AR resolution is a real inference lever for sparse series, not a tuning knob. Finer AR over-imputes effort in thinly sampled periods; the boat’s weekly-vs-monthly choice moved its catch ~20% and was the entire pooled-vs-gear-resolved discrepancy. Cap AR at what the effort series can identify.
  • The harvest is summer-extrapolation-dominated. The largest uncertainty and the largest BSS-vs-PE divergence both live in the thinly sampled months; no code refinement manufactures information that was not sampled. The single highest-value field change for 2025-26 is more summer interview/effort coverage.
  • BSS-vs-PE gaps are a diagnostic, not a defect, but demand a mechanism. The two estimate the same harvest by different routes (design-based stratum expansion vs a latent process that imputes unobserved days and propagates uncertainty). A gap is expected where sampling under-covers effort. Report the BSS (now cross-model stable for the boat) with the PE as a caveated cross-check; the boat gap is mostly the PE undercounting (P0), the shore gap is an effort-imputation tied to sigma_IE.
  • Validate by run, never by reasoning alone (the “pin” lesson). A change that looks inference-neutral on paper (the v7.3 pin; the B1.7 collapse) can perturb a delicate mass-matrix geometry. Isolate each change, compare against a confirmed baseline, and decide on pre-set criteria. Every “done pending run” item is not done until the run confirms it.
  • [2026-09-04] Never validate a change with the mechanism it is about to break. Three of the four defects in the 2026-09-03 batch share exactly that shape. The AR ladder was tested by a harness that checked bss_ar_ladder()’s return value and never that the resolution reached the data prep, so a scoped ar_escalate refit one model four times while every observable except the resolution string said the ladder worked. The ZINB feature was judged by a zero-bin diagnostic written before the mixture existed, which reported the opposite of the truth. The config comparison was validated by reading the very file it was silently truncating. The counter-measure is an assertion that reads the SOURCE of the thing under test and asserts the property that failed, not the helper’s output: 06_diagnostics/test_improvements_2026-08-25.R sections 35-37.
  • [2026-09-04] A model comparison needs the PAIRED difference SE, never the SE of either total. se_elpd_loo is dominated by across-observation variation that is common to both models and cancels in the difference; on the ZINB comparison it read 46 nats where the paired SE was 5.5, turning a 2.69 SE effect into an apparent 0.32 SE non-effect. Use 03_R_functions/loo_elpd_paired.R, and read its zero/positive decomposition: a mixture that earns its elpd at the zeros while degrading the positive counts is a different object from one that fits better everywhere. Primary source: Vehtari, Gelman & Gabry 2017, Statistics and Computing 27:1413-1432, section 3.3.
  • [2026-09-06] A gate that everything passes is not a selection rule. The AR ladder fitted shore all-gear at four resolutions and ALL FOUR passed the convergence gate, so “report the finest rung that passes” returned the finest rung RUN and decided nothing. What separated them was adequacy: p_loo as a fraction of n_obs, the Pareto k count and the PPC coverage, which moved from 0.352 / 41 / +7.1 SD at daily to 0.096 / 1 / +2.1 SD at weekly. Any ladder must therefore record adequacy for EVERY rung, not only the one it keeps (ar_rung_adequacy = TRUE); on the 2026-09-04 run two rungs cost 174 minutes and left no evidence at all.
  • [2026-09-07] A diagnostic must not be able to move a diagnostic. bss_rung_adequacy() called bss_ppc_calibration(), which reseeds, from inside the fitting loop. The C2 fit came out bit-identical to L1’s across 10,251 parameter rows and still reported a different coverage_50, because a toggle that computes extra diagnostics had shifted the RNG stream every later diagnostic drew from. Any helper added to a driver loop must save and restore .Random.seed. Bit-comparability is the tool that has caught five defects in this project and it is worth more than whatever the new helper reports.
  • [2026-09-08] A verdict must not be able to destroy the run it reads. Three batches have now aborted in the verdict block after every fit succeeded, twice losing the run’s own verdicts. The expensive part and the reading of it are different failure domains and should be wrapped as such: record the failure as a row, write what you have, and re-score from disk after the fix.
  • [2026-09-08] Compare tracks at the same resolution before calling it a disagreement. The two-track port gap widened to -1.39% when the pooled shore component moved to weekly, which looks like a divergence and is not: at a common monthly resolution the two tracks agree to 0.08%. A cross-check between models that are configured differently measures the configuration difference first.
  • [2026-09-07] Two statistics must not share a name. The same helper reported p_loo_frac as a sum over streams while model_adequacy.csv reports the worst stream’s fraction, so one run printed 2.8% and 9.6% for the same fit and invited the reader to compare them. When adding a statistic that already exists elsewhere, copy its definition or give it a different name.
  • [2026-09-06] An elpd advantage is worthless when the fit’s Pareto k have failed. The daily rung beat weekly by 85.8 nats on the effort stream, and 41 of its 311 gear observations had Pareto k above 0.7, so PSIS-LOO had failed for 13% of the very stream the comparison rested on. Read n_pareto_bad BEFORE reading elpd_loo, and read the elpd gain per effective parameter beside it: 85.8 nats for 79.7 parameters is 1.08 each, which is fitting noise.
  • [2026-09-04] Persist what the diagnostics are computed from. Three defects in five weeks lived entirely in code that runs AFTER sampling, on fits that were never wrong, and each cost a multi-hour re-fit to correct a CSV. save_ppc_draws = TRUE writes the draw objects every R-side predictive statistic reads, so the next such fix is a recomputation.
